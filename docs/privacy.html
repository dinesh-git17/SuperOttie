Privacy Policy

Super Ottie is a game, not a data business. It collects nothing about you.

The short version

No data collectedNo names, emails, locations or identifiers.
No ads or trackingNo advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
No accountNothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to.

This policy covers the Super Ottie game for iPhone (the "app") and this website. Super Ottie is made by Dinesh Dawonauth ("we", "us").

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. It has no sign-in, no in-app purchases, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking software.

The game does not need an internet connection to play, and it does not send information about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

What stays on your device

To work properly, the app saves two small things on your iPhone only:

  • Your best score, so it can be shown on the title screen.
  • Whether sound is turned on or off.

These never leave your device and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple services

You download Super Ottie through Apple's App Store or TestFlight. Apple handles those services under its own privacy policy.

If you choose to share analytics or crash reports with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may pass us anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports. If you join the TestFlight beta, Apple shares the name and email you were invited with, your device model and iOS version, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use this only to fix bugs and improve the game, and we never sell it.

Children

Super Ottie is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your choices

There is no personal data for us to access, correct or delete. To remove your saved best score and sound setting, delete the app. You can stop sharing analytics with developers at any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info@dineshd.dev.
