Meet Ottie.

Chubby, brave and never seen without those round glasses. Ottie is small, but every jump counts.

Six worlds, one otter.

Easy to pick up.

One thumb to move, one to jump. Everything else you learn by playing.

Slide to run

Keep your thumb on the stick and slide left or right. No lifting needed to turn around.

Tap to jump

Hold the button longer to jump higher.

Stomp the crabs

Land on top. Chain stomps for bigger points.

Grab the golden fish

Big Ottie breaks bricks and survives one bump.

Dodge the pufferfish

Too spiky to stomp. Jump around them.

No ads. No accounts. Nothing tracked.

Super Ottie doesn't collect any data. Your best score stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy
