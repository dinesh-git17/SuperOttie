Common questions

How do I play?

Hold your iPhone sideways. Put your left thumb on the stick and slide left or right to run. Tap the button on the right to jump, and hold it for a higher jump.

Stomp crabs from above, avoid the spiky pufferfish, and reach the flag at the end of each world.

Which devices can run it?

Super Ottie is built for iPhone and needs iOS 16 or later. It also works with a keyboard or a game controller connected to your iPhone.

Does the game save my progress?

Your best score is saved on your iPhone and shown on the title screen. Each new game starts from the first world with three lives, and a checkpoint halfway through every world means you don't restart from the beginning after a miss.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the pause button in the top corner during a level, then tap Sound. The game remembers your choice.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads, no purchases and no account to create.

How do I join the beta?

Install Apple's TestFlight app on your iPhone, then open the beta invite link. New builds arrive automatically.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

If you are on the beta, take a screenshot in the game and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback.

You can also email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened just before the problem.

How do I delete my data?

Super Ottie doesn't collect any data. The only things it stores are your best score and sound setting, on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them. See the privacy policy for details.
